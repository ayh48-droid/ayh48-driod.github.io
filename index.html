INTRODUCTION TO MARKETING

Hidden Grounds Coffee – Marketing Growth Strategy

For my Introduction to Marketing final project, my team selected Hidden Grounds Coffee as a real-world company to analyze and develop new ideas that could help the business grow.

We proposed several opportunities for the company, including bottled coffees that customers could purchase and take with them, seasonal bakery items that could create additional customer interest, and student discounts designed to attract more college students.

This project allowed me to apply marketing concepts to a real company while thinking about customer needs, product development, and potential growth opportunities.

INTRODUCTION TO MANAGEMENT

Rutgers Late-Night Food Truck

For my Introduction to Management final project, my team created a fictional business and developed a plan for how the company would operate.

We created a Rutgers-focused food truck designed to operate late at night and provide students with convenient food options when many other businesses are closed.

The business would also offer catering services for fraternity and sorority events by allowing organizations to rent the food truck for their events.

This project gave me experience thinking about business operations, target customers, services, and ways a company could generate revenue.
